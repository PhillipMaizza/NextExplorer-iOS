Privacy Policy

NextExplorer is a file browser for your own server. It keeps your files, your credentials, and your activity between your device and the server you choose, and nowhere else.

Effective 2 September 2026 App version 1.0 iOS

Short version: the developer of NextExplorer collects nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no tracking, and no third party advertising or SDKs. The app talks only to the server you point it at.

01 Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the NextExplorer iOS application (the App) handles information. It is published by Phillip Maizza (we, us).

It covers the App itself. It does not cover the server you connect the App to. That server is operated by you or by whoever administers it, under its own terms and privacy practices, which we neither control nor see.

02 How NextExplorer works

NextExplorer is a client. You supply the address of a compatible self hosted file server and sign in with your own credentials for that server. The App then browses, previews, uploads, downloads, and shares the files that live on that server.

We do not run any server on your behalf. We never receive a copy of your files, your file names, your directory structure, or your login details. Everything the App does happens either on your device or directly between your device and your server.

03 What the developer collects

Nothing. The App contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, and no third party tracking. It does not build a profile of you and does not phone home to us.

This is reflected in the App’s App Store privacy details and its bundled privacy manifest, which declare Data Not Collected and no tracking domains.

04 Data stored on your device

To work offline and stay fast, the App stores some information locally on your iPhone or iPad. This data never leaves your device except when the App sends it back to your own server, and you can erase it at any time (see Retention and deletion).

  • Server address and sessionThe server URL you enter and the authentication token or credentials for it, held in the iOS Keychain, the system’s encrypted credential store.
  • Offline directory cacheA local copy of the folder listings you have opened, saved as files on device so folders load instantly and remain viewable when the server is unreachable.
  • Thumbnail cacheDownsized preview images fetched from your server, cached on disk within a fixed size budget and evicted automatically.
  • PreferencesYour settings inside the App: appearance, date format, preview render toggles, and haptics; stored in the App’s local user defaults.

05 Data exchanged with your server

When you use the App, it communicates with the server you configured in order to list folders, fetch file contents and thumbnails, upload files you choose, create share links, and (for administrators) read or change server settings. This traffic goes directly to your server.

What that server logs or stores, and for how long, is governed by that server’s own configuration and privacy policy, not by us.

06 Device permissions

The App requests a device permission only at the moment a feature needs it, and only to carry out something you asked for:

  • Photos and files: when you choose to upload, so the item you pick can be sent to your server.
  • Local network: if your server is on your local network, iOS may ask permission to reach it.

Declining a permission only disables the specific feature that needed it; the rest of the App keeps working.

07 Third parties

The App bundles open source software components, listed with their licenses on the Open Source Licenses screen inside Settings. These components run entirely on your device and do not receive your data.

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple’s handling of your download and purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

08 Retention and deletion

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing on our side to delete. You control every copy of your data:

  • Signing out clears the stored credentials from the Keychain.
  • The App’s Settings let you clear the cached data and remove downloaded items.
  • Deleting the App removes all of its local data (caches, preferences, and Keychain items) from your device.

09 Security

Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain rather than in ordinary app storage. By default the App requires encrypted HTTPS connections to your server, in line with Apple’s App Transport Security.

If you deliberately connect to a server over an insecure or plain HTTP address, the App warns you first; in that case the protection of your data in transit depends on your own network and server, and we cannot guarantee it.

10 Children

NextExplorer is a general purpose tool for accessing your own file server and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal information at all, the App does not knowingly gather data from anyone, including children.
